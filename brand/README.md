# Forgel brand

The short version: graphite, bone and molten orange; one mark; one line.

## Name

**Forgel** — a coinage from *forge*. Capitalized in prose. Lowercase `forgel`
for the command, the package and the domain.

## Positioning

- **Category:** open-source autonomous software studio.
- **One-liner:** Forgel turns a brief into a working product on your own
  infrastructure.
- **Tagline:** *Brief in. Product out. Your cloud.*
- **Pillars:** own it, prove it, extend it, coexist.

## The mark

The letter **F** is cut from a steel billet. Its two chamfered arm ends are
still molten: the work is hot. The mark is flat and geometric — no gradient,
shadow or outline.

- Primary: bone F with molten tips on a graphite rounded square.
- Minimum size: 24 px. Clear space on every side equals the width of the F's
  stem (about 10% of the tile).
- Do not rotate, recolor or add effects. Use the mono file for one ink on
  light, the inverse file for one ink on dark.
- The avatar is a full-bleed square; GitHub applies its own crop. The glyph
  stays inside the circle at every size.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Graphite | `#14171C` | Backgrounds, primary ink on light |
| Bone | `#F2EFE9` | Mark and text on graphite |
| Molten | `#FF6A2A` | Accent, hot edges, emphasis |
| Steel | `#A7A199` | Secondary text and rules |

Molten is an accent. Do not set long body text in it on a light background.

## Type

The wordmark and the social card use **Avenir Next** (Bold). Documents and the
console use the system sans and monospace stacks; no webfont is shipped. Keep
the name in title case and the command in lowercase monospace.

## Voice

Short, concrete, no hype. Say what the product does and what it does not do.
Prefer *repository*, *staging* and *acceptance* to metaphors. No exclamation
marks; the tagline is the only slogan.
